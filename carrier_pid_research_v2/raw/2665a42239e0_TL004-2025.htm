TL004 - 2025, TransCentral Sunset and ContainerLINK SolutionCenter

Released: May 28, 2025

<-- Back  |  View in pdf format

As part of an ongoing process to streamline our digital footprint, Carrier Transicold will be retiring the TransCentral portal and legacy PartsNet (partsorder.com) application.

In its place, we are introducing the ContainerLINK SolutionCenter website (https://carrier-global.my.site.com/Container/). With a little cleanup, the TransCentral information is being carried over to the new website.

This would include software releases, software history, TechLINEs and service bulletins. Also, within the website you will have a centralized access point to the other Carrier container websites. If there is a file or bulletin you cannot find, please inform your field service area manager.

The ContainerLINK SolutionCenter is accessed via the Okta Single Sign-On used to access the Ecommerce system. If you require access to ContainerLINK SolutionCenter, send an email to [email protected] to request access to the new site.
